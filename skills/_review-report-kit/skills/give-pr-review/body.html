Agree
Post the draft as written. The card gets a green edge and an agreed chip, and stays listed until you mark it posted.
Drop

Copy buttons
Each card has its own Copy button. Copy new comments (bottom bar) exports what you haven't copied yet; Copy all resends everything. Cards in that next batch carry a to copy chip in their header - hover the button to outline them, or use Not copied yet to list them.

After Agree or Drop the page moves on to the next open card. Click a pressed Agree or Drop again to undo it and reopen the card. Post each comment on GitHub yourself, then tell Claude "posted 2, 5" (or just re-run the report). Decisions and notes are saved in this browser.
